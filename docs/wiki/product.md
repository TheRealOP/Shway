# Product

## Promise

Express an edit with your voice, hands, and references. AI handles routine operations while the
creator keeps the decisions and access to precise editing tools.

## Primary workflow

Import alternative speaking takes and supporting footage → describe the story and desired feeling
→ watch a real first cut → twist to scrub → gesture to reject a take and speak the reason
→ preview a replacement → accept or undo → export.

The first cut must contain actual source-backed edits, not a simulated video-editor screen.
The creator can inspect and refine all changes through Filmcraft's normal tools.

## Initial demo

Use a 20–40 second story with three original speaking takes and a little supporting footage.
Demonstrate one successful local replacement such as:
“That sounds rehearsed; use the take where I laugh, then show the result.”

After that works, add one exposure adjustment, dialogue-aware music refinement,
one corrected gesture sensitivity profile, and TRIBE analysis of actual rendered versions
(TRIBE is tabled for the hackathon; see [Decisions](decisions.md)).

## Creative intent

Capture intended message, audience, beats, feeling, duration, required moments, references,
and exclusions in an editable StoryBrief. Words, pointing, selection, and motion contribute context.
A multimodal LLM interprets these signals; the user can correct its interpretation.

TRIBE predicts neural responses to stimuli. It does not read a creator's private thoughts or
establish which edit will cause a particular emotion.

## Scope boundary

Full professional-tool parity is inherited from the editor where verified, not a claim that every
upstream feature has been tested. Generative relighting, autonomous broad story reconstruction,
reference-response matching, full Harness² integration, and phone/EMG inputs are later work.
